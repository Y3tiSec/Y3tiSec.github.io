Welcome to Y3ti@Sec!

We started as a small group of curious minds, asking “What if?” What if we pushed ourselves beyond limits? What if we created a space to learn, break, fix, and repeat?

Now we’re Y3ti@Sec, born in Nepal built for the cyber world. From CTFs to real-world exploits, we’re here to make noise. And trust us the future’s listening.

“The future will remember our name.”

Our Growing Mission

Building Community

Connecting people and creating a strong cybersecurity network in Nepal.

Learning Together

No one learns alone here as we share ideas and grow through teamwork.

Future Leaders

Empowering the next generation to lead the way in cybersecurity.

Our Services

We’re a group of students who build custom CTF challenges based on what we know best.
We also provide our own platform to host CTF events and help you run them smoothly from start to finish. Besides that, we do pentesting too as students, we love putting our skills to the test to help you find and fix security issues.


If you’d like to work with us, host a CTF, or need a pentest, feel free to reach out to any of our founding members or email us at y3tisec@gmail.com we’d love to hear from you!
